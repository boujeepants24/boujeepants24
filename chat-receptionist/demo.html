This is any client's existing website.

The chat button in the corner is the receptionist. It answers at 2am, sorts emergencies from routine jobs, collects the name, number, area and problem, and drops the lead straight into the client's sheet and inbox.

Try typing naturally, for example my boiler says F28 or water is pouring through the ceiling.

Install on a client's site

<script src="https://YOUR-HOST/clients/northfield/config.js"></script>
<script src="https://YOUR-HOST/widget.js" defer></script>

It works with WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify and plain HTML: anywhere you can paste a script tag.
